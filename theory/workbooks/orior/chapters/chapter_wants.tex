A want is a posit, stated in the terms of the field it reaches into, beside the open question between

\section{Absolute zero as the noise floor}

\textbf{Posit.} Absolute zero is the noise floor of physical measurement: the reference state every
structure is read against. It is not an empty state.

\begin{itemize}
\item Classical thermodynamics treats $T = 0$ K as the end of motion. In quantum field theory a system
cooled to $T = 0$ keeps its state space and sits in its ground state, where zero-point fluctuations
persist.

\item Thermal entropy $S_{\mathrm{thermal}} = k_B \ln \Omega$ goes to zero for a non-degenerate ground
state, as the third law requires. The state space $\Sigma$ stays fully populated: at every coordinate
$g \in G$ the field operator keeps a nonzero expectation value $x(g)$. The posit holds that the
vacuum's entanglement entropy across a bipartition is maximal.

\item By the Nernst unattainability principle $T = 0$ is reached only as a limit, after infinitely
many steps. Absolute zero is therefore a reference state, in which thermal uncertainty is replaced
entirely by irreducible zero-point noise. It is not an informational vacuum.

\item The posit maps the anchored search onto the two regimes:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
search & thermal region, $T > 0$ & absolute zero, $T = 0$ \\
\midrule
field state $x(g)$ & thermal fluctuations and quantum noise & ground-state vacuum fluctuations \\
alphabet $\Sigma$ & thermal occupation modes & zero-point vacuum modes \\
indicator $\delta_0$ & comparisons blurred by thermal noise & exact matching of quantum states \\
pruning $\partial A$ & dissipative, Landauer cost above zero & pruning that preserves entanglement \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\item The domain map $x : G \to \Sigma$ stays defined at every coordinate. The elements of $\Sigma$ go
from thermal mixed states to pure ground-state field amplitudes.

\item The ground state is the search's maximum-entropy baseline: the field without structure, before
an anchor set $A$ is applied to read local structural correlations.

\item Lowering the temperature removes thermal noise without removing the background data. The
anchors $\mathrm{Anc}_A$ and the field's correlation functions are then resolved by the equality
operator $\delta_0$. Absolute zero is the fundamental noise floor: the maximum-entropy background every
physical measurement is taken against.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What stands open.} The vacuum's entanglement entropy across a region follows an area law, set
by the region's boundary, and is not maximal~\cite{src:Srednicki-1993}. Nothing in this repository
measures a physical ground state, and the mapping onto the anchored search is an analogy until a
measurement of the search depends on it.

\section{Unification and the continuum}

\textbf{Posit.} The universe has no initial singularity. It passes through a smooth, non-singular
bounce from a previous universe, and relativity follows from the density of information in a
continuous medium. Its parts, in order:

\textbf{A. No initial singularity.} Run backward, general relativity takes the universe to a single
point, where the theory breaks down. The posit holds that the energy cost of compressing information
forbids that point. A Planck-epoch compression of everything into one point cannot happen, and the
universe instead passes through a global phase inversion.

\begin{itemize}
\item The cost of packing information rises with its density, through the quantum noise between the
groupings being packed (here called informational viscosity). Packing all of the universe's
information into one sub-Planckian coordinate would need the quantum foam packed perfectly, and the
cost of that diverges.

\item Before the cost diverges, the medium reaches a maximum information capacity (here called the
cosmic breaker). The collapse stops at that bound and does not reach a singularity. The bound comes
from the same physics as everything else, and no separate rule is needed to exclude the singular
state.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{B. The bounce.} The universe passes smoothly between two macroscopic states, and the
information entropy is inverted between them.

\begin{itemize}
\item The previous universe expanded until its information entropy reached its maximum $H_{\max}$.
It dissolved into unstructured sub-Planckian noise: a field that is uniform apart from its noise.

\item At complete uniformity there are no distinguishable coordinates, distances or particles, and
scale has no meaning. Uniform randomness at cosmic scale cannot be told from uniform randomness below
the Planck scale.

\item The previous universe's complete dispersion is the initial state of ours (here called the
phoenix effect). The maximum entropy of the old universe is the maximum potential of the new. Matter
was not compressed. The coordinate system inverted.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{C. The low-entropy beginning.} The past hypothesis asks why our universe began in a highly
ordered, low-entropy state~\cite{src:Albert-2000}. Standard cosmology takes it as an initial
condition. The posit holds that it follows necessarily from continuum mechanics: our universe began
ordered because it inherited a fully reset, uniform sub-Planckian state from the previous one.

\textbf{D. The cosmology in four statements.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The medium is a smooth continuum below the Planck scale.
\item Information is a physical, fluid concentration moving through noise.
\item Physical law is computed locally, at every point.
\item Time is emergent. It is the direction in which information entropy increases, the
thermodynamic arrow, as the global inversion plays out.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{E. Relativity from information density.} Gravity and velocity are both measures of how densely
information is packed locally, and that density sets the rate at which the field updates its state.
The field's parallel computation is very large even at one update a second, and it runs everywhere at
once.

\begin{itemize}
\item Mass is information congestion. A particle or a mass is a localized knot or eddy, a dense
concentration of the field's data. A dense concentration slows the rate at which information
dissipates, as a heavy drag does in a fluid. That rate is time, and gravitational time dilation is the
field taking longer to update a dense state.

\item Velocity is structural compression. A body moving through the medium near the speed of light is
compressed against the oncoming field, which raises its local information density and slows the rate
at which its entropy increases. An outside observer sees time dilation and length contraction. Where
relativity says space stretches, the posit says the field's data is compressed.

\item The speed of light $c$ is the saturation threshold of the medium: the maximum rate at which the
field passes information from one point to the next. Past that density or rate the field closes into a
black hole. A black hole is the ceiling of local information density.

\item The posit introduces no constant without a mechanism. Each constant is a property the medium
already has, and a derivation finds it instead of setting it.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What stands open.} The posit gives no equation, and a test needs one first: the Lorentz
transformations and the field equations of general relativity derived from information density. The
step at maximum entropy, where scale loses its meaning and the end of one universe becomes the start
of the next, is the premise of conformal cyclic cosmology~\cite{src:Penrose-2010}. Gravity derived
from information and entropy is the program of Jacobson~\cite{src:Jacobson-1995} and
Verlinde~\cite{src:Verlinde-2011}. A maximum information density, reached by a black hole, is the
Bekenstein bound~\cite{src:Bekenstein-1981}. A maximum speed at which a local medium carries
information is the Lieb--Robinson bound~\cite{src:Lieb-and-Robinson-1972}. The universe as a
computation whose rate is set by its energy is Lloyd's~\cite{src:Lloyd-2002}. The posit has to be set
against each of them.

\section{Suppositions, by the hour}

The notebook is kept in the order the thinking happened. Entries 1 and 10 (tail error), 4 to 6
(doping), 7 (proteins) and 17 (cultural material) are stated as hypotheses or as a constraint in the
thought experiment. Entry 13's demon is answered there by the delta null: the boundary refuses and
does not predict. The other entries stand here as wants. An approximate representation of an object,
written appxrep, is a representation of it as vectors and magnitudes, held without a bound on its
order.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Tail error correction is working. Adding a periodic perturbation drives the joint error on an
undisturbed co-arm toward zero by several orders of magnitude.

\item This correction is another appxrep, and like the others it should be left unbounded.

\item Crystallography. A grain boundary and a grain size are instances of the boundaries every natural
process leaves. Each has its own boundary curve, shaped by the physical processes that bound it and by
the conditions while it formed, and any gradient of scarcity in those conditions records where it
formed.

\item Crystallography. A dopant in a host lattice plays the part a salt plays in a hash, above all a
known dopant: its distribution has already been measured.

\item Crystallography. It follows that the complement of the set of measured arrangements is
determined as well, and the difference between the two is a real quantity.

\item Crystallography. Posit: from measured dopant distributions, new allowed dopant arrangements can
be predicted.

\item Biology. The Ramachandran plot gives the allowed backbone conformations of a protein, and any
structure known to exist can be held as an appxrep. It follows that a protein held as vectors and
magnitudes is a natural representation and keeps the order of assembly. Posit: if the known allowed
structures exist, the complement of the allowed set exists and is a subset of the superset. If the
posit holds, it predicts chirality and new allowed structures.

\item Salishan. The hand extraction comes first. After it, write specific hypotheses that ask the
corpus narrower questions and exclude more of the unbounded set: tailored amplification and tuned
nulls. There is a natural complex curve, and the closer it sits to the imaginary axis, the more
information can be extracted. The curve is a function, and it cannot be defined until the extraction is
finished.

\item Conjecture. The universe is a set that exists and can be known, and every subset of it
necessarily exists. Any set can be represented approximately, with its information conserved. An
unbounded, undefined set of $n$ dimensions can be used to ask questions of other unbounded, undefined
sets of $n$ dimensions, and such an object encodes everything about itself in itself. Bounding the
object under examination adds information that is not in it and destroys its unbounded power to
discriminate. Consummatio omnino pulchra est.

\item Tail error correction works best with a random perturbation that has no affine structure. Error
reduction against percent inclusion has not been plotted. It is a recursive function of the tail, and
the prediction is a smooth natural curve. A straight line would show a branch that could reach a tail
error of $10^{-26}$.

\item Posit. A noise error-correcting code learned in one domain carries its affine regularity to
another through field transforms. Seen from the data, the transforms are mutations. Operationally the
code is a dimensionless field transform, and it fails under bounding. Every transform has to keep
that property, so that no dimension is lost. The data is mutated, which conserves its unbounded
variability, while a transformation aims at a goal other than asking the data a question, and that
goal introduces information that is not in the data. A mutation has no goal. It is random, and random
noise cancels itself on inversion.

\item Self-detection needs no reflexive loop and no separate layer. It is an appxrep difference
composed with a null permutation. Treating self-observation as a dimensional transform avoids the
paradoxes of self-reference: Gödel sentences, infinite regress and the halting problem.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The null permutation $\pi_0$. A state transition applies a permutation $\pi$ to the state,
$S_{t+1} = \pi(S_t)$. The null permutation is the identity on the active transform axes. Executing it
does no structural work on the data, and the operator still registers the boundary condition.
Self-detection is a transition whose net permutation is zero on every operational dimension.

\item The appxrep difference as a projection. The ground set $\Sigma$ can discriminate without bound.
Projecting a state onto a lower-dimensional appxrep therefore leaves a difference between the state and its
projection, $\Delta_{\mathrm{self}} = S \setminus \mathrm{appxrep}(S)$. The differencing is applied
across dimensions instead of across clock ticks, and it needs no external monitor and no stack of
nested interpreters. Differencing across time measures how the state evolves, $S_t \to S_{t+1}$.
Differencing across dimensions measures the difference between the unagglomerated superset noise
$S_{\max}$ and the compressed projection of the state held in memory.

\item Self-detection reduces to $\mathrm{appxrep}(\Delta) \circ \pi_0$. The machine therefore uses the same
code paths, registers and set operations for observing itself as for its primary computation.
\end{enumerate}

\item Laplace's demon is reached through a topological boundary and Jaynes's
proposition~\cite{src:Jaynes}. The proposition is a boundary condition present in $\Sigma$, and it
realizes the holographic principle: the information of a three-dimensional volume can be encoded on
its two-dimensional boundary~\cite{src:Susskind-1995}. The universe is a set $\Sigma$, the
measurement is $\Delta_{\mathrm{null}}$, and the engine is the topological boundary.

\item The algorithm is about five days old at this entry, and nearly all of that time has gone into
reading Salishan. The Salishan corpus still has a long way to go, and no error may be introduced into

\item Performance. Tail accuracy is now the largest target, and it has to reach its floor much faster.
Once its mathematics is settled, the engine is to be tuned for superscalar hosts, with the assembly
breakdown kept for the teaching material that comes with the engine. It is to be written four ways:
full assembly; pure C23 SWAR at machine word width; fractional, with a branch that follows a baseline;
and wider buses where the hardware accelerates them. This waits for the hand extraction of Salishan.

\item Causality bounds the sampling rate, and today the hardware sets that bound. Between samples the
information varies without bound, as a continuum. Posit: the lower bound on the sampling interval is
the Planck time, which can be approached and never met unless latency and propagation time are
removed from the universe. If they are always present and predictable, materials science can speed up
their physical function, and the time between samples can be filled with a delta, so that time itself
is not needed. If the engine works as designed, it records every transient to a tail error of
$10^{-26}$. State information is encoded in the object's entropy, and conservation of energy requires
that it is.

\item A cultural object cannot be reconstructed unless the whole living object is encoded in the object
itself. Repeating patterns, counterexamples from teaching, and fragments of the same protoculture
surviving at the edges of collapsed civilizations regularly help fill cultural gaps. Where something
was never recorded, determining its meaning takes much more care, and meaning is never injected where
there is none. That widens the base set to include the unknown: more is included, which means more
data for each complete set. Storing unbounded variability is not possible with conventional methods,
since every variable costs cycles, memory or both. The algorithm is to use the whole of Salishan, from
the whole input corpus, to recover faded text, damaged art, and the distribution and concentration of
populations, combining archaeological work with known measures of distance between languages.
Archaeologists and linguists already derive these. Anthropology may have known it longest, since it is
the field's own thought exercise: if A moved from B, knowing from archaeological evidence what lay
between, would the move be possible under any human circumstance? Anthropology considers aspects of
human movement other fields leave alone. A subject that was never written down lives only as an idea,
mother to daughter, father to son, community to family, society to community, and if all of those are
gone it cannot be recovered. For that reason the tool is not to be used to regenerate anything without
a human representative of that culture. The information is not thereby gone. Gone is the ability
to tell a true statement, a member of its set, from an untrue one. Representing something untrue
about a culture as true would be a grave ethical violation, and it would destroy the culture itself.
This proof therefore only examines what is already there and asks whether it recreated it. It does not
ask where the edges of what is known lie. The tools for that exist. Their author built them and does
not use them for that.
\end{enumerate}
