\section{\texorpdfstring{Long descriptions}{Long descriptions}}

The root noise of a box (E16 in \hyperref[chap:engine-table]{engine\_\allowbreak{}table.md}) reads rows 8, 9, 10, 18 and 25 as patterns in fewer dimensions than an object's box. A term's pattern is the box's mean along the axes it shares, rounded down; its residual is the box less the pattern spread back. The crystal's coder prices the box, each residual and each pattern, and the term that saves the most bits is the root, yanked with its residual and its pattern, from which the box returns exactly (\texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}root\_\allowbreak{}box}, \texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}root\_\allowbreak{}return}). The \texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}root} sim (72 checks, 0 failed, \texttt{build/\allowbreak{}20260925\_\allowbreak{}170854\_\allowbreak{}sim\_\allowbreak{}noise\_\allowbreak{}root}, a tessera job) plants each term alone at variance 64, 256 and 1,024 in a box of 16 frames × 12 × 32 × 32 around a standing body, where a voxel's independent variance is 104. The null names no root; in every planted run the planted term is the root or there is none, never another term; at 1,024 every term is the root. The savings below are per mille of the box's bits, which run 1,194,939 to 1,362,651.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Photon shot.} From the four vectors, the draft (ξ\_\allowbreak{}shot) and compression's categories. Independent every frame; its variance grows with the level. Read per level bin, 40 to 199 with 100,000 pairs or more, at lag 1 (\texttt{transfer\_\allowbreak{}residual.\allowbreak{}py}) by the neighbor route, E[(d − d\_\allowbreak{}x)²]/4: its slope is g·F², its intercept R² less g·O plus every white level-free term (C3, C5, C7). Camera law, built: a Poisson count whose first four cumulants are each S (\texttt{sim\_\allowbreak{}poisson\_\allowbreak{}four\_\allowbreak{}cumulants}, \texttt{SIM\_\allowbreak{}SHOT\_\allowbreak{}POISSON}, sim\_\allowbreak{}camera.h), with Binomial(4S, ½) − S, symmetric, kept beside it as \texttt{SIM\_\allowbreak{}SHOT\_\allowbreak{}SYMMETRIC}; the \texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}floor} sim recovers slope 2.0046 against a planted 2, and it draws the Poisson law (noise\_\allowbreak{}floor.cu). On the kcr set: slope 0.8168 to 1.1540, the worst bin within ±23‰ of its line on 19 of 24, and 63 to 109‰ on 0c582fdc, 12dfb391, 341df25f and 3a861e03. The raw route, E[d²]/2, reads slope 1.0156 to 5.7623 and bends (row 26).

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The cumulant ladder} (\texttt{track\_\allowbreak{}driver -\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}run moments}; each quiet static voxel's frames in blocks of 5, about the block's mean; \texttt{moments\_\allowbreak{}ladder.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{ladder\_\allowbreak{}against\_\allowbreak{}neighbour.\allowbreak{}py}). A Poisson count scaled by g, with symmetric read noise added, holds κ₂ slope g, κ₃ slope g², κ₄ slope g³. With g from the neighbor route, on the 12 clean samples with a static ceiling under 160 and no bulk floor clip, κ₃ runs 1.3 to 4.6 times the rung g² and κ₄ 13.6 to 43.2 times g³ (415c0a3a's κ₄ reads −27.63). On 1574802b, from level 7 to 34, κ₃/κ₂ holds at 1.49 to 1.71 while κ₄/κ₂ climbs from 2.0 to 13.6. The background's fluctuation is the shot plus a positive tail not yet named; it sits in Ω (row 30). Candidates the frames can't split: structure passing through the dimmest quarter, which only brightens; a multiplying mechanism in the camera (an electron-multiplying register holds κ₃ = 3G·κ₂, which the readings don't hold); sparse bright events (spurious charge at g near 1 is far too small).
\item \textbf{What sits inside the shot.} A count drawn at a rate that itself moves is a mixed Poisson (Cox) count, whose factorial cumulants are the rate's ordinary cumulants. Rows 3, 7, 10, 11, 19 and 20 move the rate before the draw; rows 4, 5, 6, 17, 22 and 23 act after it. Reading factorial cumulants needs the count in electrons, and it waits on g apart (row 12).
\item \textbf{The shot as many coherent pieces.} With M modes in one voxel's exposure the variance is g·L + L²/M. The neighbor route fitted as a + bL + cL² (\texttt{transfer\_\allowbreak{}curvature.\allowbreak{}py}) gives c within ±3 × 10⁻³ on all 24, positive on 13 and negative on 11, some many standard errors from 0: something besides modes bends it, and no mode count is read. κ₄ is the free check of the many-piece limit, and it fails upward.
\item The ladder in the sim, built: \texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}ladder\_\allowbreak{}volume} reads each quiet static block's k₂, k₃ and k₄ against its voxel's level. In \texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}terms}, on the Poisson shot with gain 2, offset 20 and read variance 4: s₂ 1.9852 within 1/50 of g, s₃ 3.9933 within 1/4 of g², s₄ 9.4410 within 3 of g³, O 19.9533 within 3 and R² 4.1981 within 6, each reach about 5 standard errors. Next: the positive tail named; it sits in Ω (row 30).
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Dark current level.} From the draft (D, I\_\allowbreak{}dark·Δt). Constant in time at a fixed exposure; grows with the exposure time Δt. Dark electrons enter before the gain, as light does, and the mean dark charge g·D·Δt moves a voxel along the transfer line and never off it (C14 in compression\_\allowbreak{}table.md). The neighbor route's intercept holds −g·O, the electronic offset added after the gain, and none of the dark level. On the kcr set every sample is 100 frames at one exposure time, with no dark frames and no bias frames, and the dark level is read as light. Camera law, built: dark electrons D·Δt a frame (\texttt{dark}), drawn with the light by the shot law before the gain (sim\_\allowbreak{}camera.h). \texttt{noise\_\allowbreak{}charge\_\allowbreak{}series} reads a dark series' mean less a bias series' as g·D·Δt: 32.0002 and 63.9988 against 32 and 64, within 1/100. Next: a bias and a dark series from the camera itself (open item 4).
\item \textbf{Dark current shot.} From the list asked for and the draft (D inside the root). Independent every frame; variance g²·D·Δt in DN², on the same line as photon shot. At one exposure its variance comes with its own mean g·D·Δt, on the transfer line (C14), and it is read as light, not in the intercept. At zero light, a dark frame's E[d²]/2 less a bias frame's is g²·D·Δt, and its mean less a bias frame's is g·D·Δt; their ratio is g. The kcr set holds neither frame. Camera law, built: the shot draws the light's and the dark's electrons together (sim\_\allowbreak{}camera.h). The sim reads g²·D·Δt 64.0040 and 127.9703 against 64 and 128, within 1/5, and g, their ratio, 2.0001 and 1.9995, within 1/300. Next: dark and bias frames from the camera itself (open item 4).
\item \textbf{Read.} From the four vectors (“thermal and read”), the draft (σ²\_\allowbreak{}read) and compression's categories. Independent every frame; does not depend on the level. Read by the neighbor route's intercept (C7). \item \textbf{Thermal (Johnson–Nyquist).} From the four vectors and the draft's classification (“Thermal/Read”). Independent every frame; does not depend on the level; grows with temperature. \item \textbf{Clock-induced / spurious charge.} From the list asked for and the draft's classification (CIC, under multiplicative). Sparse single-electron events at a rate p per voxel per frame, before any gain register. Read by spikes against dips at 16, 32, 64 and 128 in the static voxels (\texttt{track\_\allowbreak{}driver -\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}run clips}, noise\_\allowbreak{}spikes.tsv). Camera law, built: an excess F² = 2, twice a binomial half of the count (sim\_\allowbreak{}camera.h); after the thinning g·f′ reads 3.9993 and 3.9981 against 4. Next: row 1's tail named.

\item \textbf{Partition.} From the list asked for. A binomial split of the carriers: quantum efficiency as thinning, or charge shared between wells. \item \textbf{Fixed pattern (DSNU).} From the four vectors, compression's categories and the draft (η\_\allowbreak{}spatial). Constant in time at a pixel; cancels exactly in d (C3). Each (y, x) is one camera pixel in every z plane; the pixel pass sums each pixel's static voxels in the lower and upper halves of z (\texttt{track\_\allowbreak{}driver -\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}run pixels}, noise\_\allowbreak{}pixels.tsv; \texttt{pixel\_\allowbreak{}analysis.\allowbreak{}py}, maps less their 3 × 3 neighbors' median). \section{\texorpdfstring{The notation}{The notation}}

A voxel v sits at (z, y, x) and holds I\_\allowbreak{}t(v) in frame t, in DN. Its level L is its value's mean over the frames it is read in. The frame difference is d\_\allowbreak{}t(v) = I\_\allowbreak{}\{t+1\}(v) − I\_\allowbreak{}t(v). Following compression\_\allowbreak{}table C7, the gain g is DN per electron, and the draft's G is its inverse (electrons per DN). O is the lane's value at zero light. Every reading below is a sum of integer products over named voxels and frames. Two ratios are compared by multiplying across (a·d against b·c), as the period's 128-bit test does. A spread is compared as its square, with no root, and a code length is a bit length, with no logarithm.

The composite each voxel's frame difference integrates, with structure taken off by the neighbor (C5):

E[d²] / 2 = F²·g·(L − O) + R² + σ²\_\allowbreak{}thermal + σ²\_\allowbreak{}kTC + σ²\_\allowbreak{}row + σ²\_\allowbreak{}col + σ²\_\allowbreak{}1/f(1) + σ²\_\allowbreak{}CIC + 1/12 + Ω(v)

with F the excess noise factor, R² the read variance and 1/12 the quantization of a unit step (held as 12·σ² to stay integer). The fixed pattern and the dark level cancel in d (C3). The dark current's shot rides the same line as the photon shot, since the dark electrons are counted in L. PRNU scales g per voxel instead of adding a term. Optical crosstalk blurs S before the draw and adds no noise term. Electrical crosstalk adds covariance between neighbors, not variance at one voxel. Row 10 is a scale ε shared by the whole volume, not an added draw: it adds ε²·L² to the raw route, and the neighbor difference removes it. Ω is the open term.

\section{\texorpdfstring{What the integrated floor is for}{What the integrated floor is for}}

\textbf{Compression.} Each named row's predicted spread at a voxel's level gives that voxel's counts, and the coder spends Σ −log₂ p against them (compression\_\allowbreak{}table F5). The bits go to departure and to Ω. When a set needs a category the rows lack, the gap to F5 names it, and it enters Ω first. On the 15 clean samples of the kcr set, the coherent terms measured here (static rows, columns and plane, and the z correlation) are worth 1.0\% to 3.0\% of the static background's frame-difference bits, which run 4.71 to 6.04 bits per voxel-frame (a Gaussian estimate, \texttt{coherent\_\allowbreak{}bits.\allowbreak{}py}). The independent shot and read are the rest.

\textbf{Filtering.} A voxel-frame whose d departs from the integrated floor by more than the null allows is departure: a body, a split, a drive (noise\_\allowbreak{}sieve\_\allowbreak{}tower §17). The null draws read the rate against the integrated floor, not against one sample-wide reading.
