\section{Byte-Pair Distribution Method}

\textbf{Purpose:} Run the byte-pair distribution pipeline: squash text to a flat distribution, measure distance, establish validity by split-half, correct for support, cluster, and control for family. \textbf{Scope:} \texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}byte\_\allowbreak{}signature.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{tools/\allowbreak{}Salishan/\allowbreak{}corpus\_\allowbreak{}script\_\allowbreak{}extraction/\allowbreak{}salish\_\allowbreak{}purity.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{tools/\allowbreak{}instrument/\allowbreak{}corpus\_\allowbreak{}gate.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}case\_\allowbreak{}or\_\allowbreak{}splitting.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}cluster\_\allowbreak{}profiles.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}evidential\_\allowbreak{}pressure.\allowbreak{}py}

\subsection{1. Pair Encoding}

Text \(T\), whitespace stripped, encoded UTF-8 to bytes \(b_0 \dots b_{m-1}\). Every adjacent pair maps to one index:

\(k_i \;=\; 256\,b_i + b_{i+1}, \qquad k_i \in [0,\,65536)\)

This is the squash. A variable-length sequence over a \(256\)-symbol alphabet becomes a fixed flat array of \(2^{16}\) cells, with no character-level decision taken anywhere in it. The map is injective on pairs, so no two distinct pairs collide.

\(P_T(k) \;=\; \frac{c_T(k)}{\sum_{j} c_T(j)}, \qquad c_T(k) = \bigl|\{\, i : k_i = k \,\}\bigr|\)

\(P_T\) is a probability distribution over \(2^{16}\) outcomes (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}byte\_\allowbreak{}signature.\allowbreak{}py:85-\allowbreak{}93}).

\subsection{2. Total Variation}

\(D(P,Q) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2} \sum_{k=0}^{65535} \bigl| P(k) - Q(k) \bigr| \;\in\; [0,1]\)

Total variation. \(D=0\) when the distributions agree everywhere, \(D=1\) when their supports are disjoint (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}byte\_\allowbreak{}signature.\allowbreak{}py:96-\allowbreak{}99}).

\subsection{3. Split-Half Resolution}

\(D(P_A,P_B)\) between two corpora means nothing until you know the resolution of the estimator at that sample size. Split each corpus at its midpoint and measure it against itself:

\(D_{\text{self}}(T) \;=\; D\bigl(P_{T[0:m/2]},\; P_{T[m/2:m]}\bigr)\)

A between-corpus reading is interpretable when

\(D_{\text{self}}(T) \;\ll\; D(P_T, P_{T'}) \quad \forall\, T' \neq T\)

and is uninterpretable when \(D_{\text{self}}(T) > \max_{T'} D_{\text{self}}(T')\), which says the corpus is too small to distinguish from noise (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}byte\_\allowbreak{}signature.\allowbreak{}py:162-\allowbreak{}196}).

Measured on 20 corpora cut to 6707 bytes each: \(D_{\text{self}}\) ranged \(0.1118\) to \(0.3192\), mean \(0.2361\).

\subsection{4. Support and Entropy Correction}

\(D_{\text{self}}\) falls as the distribution concentrates, independently of the language. Report support and entropy alongside it:

\(\mathrm{supp}(P) = \bigl|\{\,k : P(k) > 0\,\}\bigr|, \qquad H(P) = -\sum_{k} P(k)\log_2 P(k)\)

Across those 20 corpora, \(\mathrm{supp}\) ran \(193\) to \(970\) and tracked \(D_{\text{self}}\) almost monotonically. A corpus in a low-support encoding looks internally consistent for arithmetic reasons. Any ranking of \(D_{\text{self}}\) across different writing systems is a ranking of \(\mathrm{supp}\) until shown otherwise (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}byte\_\allowbreak{}signature.\allowbreak{}py:157-\allowbreak{}175}).

\subsection{5. Extraction Corruption}

Given the character sets \(S = \{s_1 \dots s_q\}\) that carry the distinctive parts of a writing system:

\(\mathrm{sets}(T) = \sum_{s \in S} \mathbf{1}\bigl[\,\exists\, c \in T,\ c \in s\,\bigr], \qquad \mu(T) = \frac{10^3}{|T_\alpha|}\bigl|\{\,c \in T : \mathrm{comb}(c)\,\}\bigr|\)

where \(T_\alpha\) is the alphabetic subsequence and \(\mathrm{comb}\) marks combining characters. Extraction loss drops combining marks first, so \(\mu \to 0\) with \(\mathrm{sets} \ll q\) identifies a corrupted file whose surviving text is still well-formed (\texttt{tools/\allowbreak{}Salishan/\allowbreak{}corpus\_\allowbreak{}script\_\allowbreak{}extraction/\allowbreak{}salish\_\allowbreak{}purity.\allowbreak{}py:57-\allowbreak{}71}).

Run on 60 extracted papers with \(q=7\): one file scored \(\mathrm{sets} \ge 5\) with \(\mu > 0\). The rest lost the marks entirely.

\subsection{6. Permutation Null}

To separate a real partition from the arithmetic of splitting a key space, permute the labels within each pre-split group, holding cell counts fixed:

\(\pi^{\ast} \sim \mathrm{Unif}\bigl(\mathcal{S}(\text{labels within group})\bigr), \qquad \Delta = \mathrm{E}\bigl[\,r(\pi^{\ast})\,\bigr] - r(\pi_{\text{obs}})\)

\(\Delta \le 0\) means the observed split bought nothing a random split of identical shape would not (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}case\_\allowbreak{}or\_\allowbreak{}splitting.\allowbreak{}py:110-\allowbreak{}131}). Over 19 corpora capped to 60000 tokens, \(\Delta\) ranged \(+0.0056\) to \(+0.0901\), with null scatter \(\le 0.0133\) over 5 draws.

\subsection{7. Cophenetic Validation}

Average-linkage agglomeration over \(D\). A tree can be built from any matrix, so report the correlation between the cophenetic height \(h(i,j)\) at which \(i\) and \(j\) first join and the measured distance:

\(\rho_c \;=\; \mathrm{corr}\bigl(\,D(i,j),\; h(i,j)\,\bigr)_{i < j}\)

Low \(\rho_c\) means the tree imposes structure the distances do not carry, and the output is an ordering with no groups in it (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}cluster\_\allowbreak{}profiles.\allowbreak{}py}). Measured \(\rho_c = 0.8406\) over \(171\) pairs on 19 languages.

\subsection{8. Family-Stratified Sampling}

Languages are not independent draws. Sampling one per family defeats the shared-history term:

\(\hat{\pi} \;=\; \frac{1}{d}\sum_{t=1}^{d} \frac{1}{|F|}\sum_{f \in F} \mathbf{1}\bigl[\,h(x_{f,t})\,\bigr], \qquad x_{f,t} \sim \mathrm{Unif}(f)\)

over \(d\) draws and family set \(F\). Report \(\hat{\pi}\) with its across-draw standard deviation (\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}language/\allowbreak{}4\_\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}evidential\_\allowbreak{}pressure.\allowbreak{}py:65-\allowbreak{}78}). Run on 418 languages across 121 families, \(d=300\): area rates \(0.089\) to \(0.716\) with scatter \(0.029\) to \(0.069\).
