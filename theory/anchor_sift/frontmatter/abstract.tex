\chapter*{Abstract}

Measure how far an object sits from the most disordered arrangement of its own parts.

That is the construction. Every domain in this book is that one sentence with a different answer to
what counts as a part: atoms in a cell, symbols in a corpus, bytes in a file, coordinates in a board
layout. The reference is built from the object's own parts, so there is no prior to estimate, no
training set to collect, and no model of the domain to write.

Two propositions bound what the search half can do. Any subset of a pattern's points is a necessary
condition for the pattern, so a filter built from a subset never loses a true occurrence, and that
holds whatever picked the subset, whatever the alphabet is, and whether or not positions are
ordered. The converse fails for every proper subset, so the exact compare is irreducible and every
arm has to verify what its anchors admitted. Soundness is free; the reading is not.

Two instruments do the measuring, and they are not interchangeable as evidence. The shift agreement
detector recovers a period by how often a shift agrees with itself, and it has external ground
truth: 453 of 453 axes of published crystal cell edges, every one recovered inside a single voxel,
mean absolute error 0.0124~\AA. The permutation null measure reads a departure from the maximum
entropy arrangement of the same multiset. It carries most of the findings in this work and it has no
positive control from outside. Three separate claims here merged the two instruments and each was
corrected after the fact.

The negative result is the sharper one. Collision entropy is invariant under permutation, so a
corpus and its own shuffle carry identical values, exactly and not approximately. No bound built
from a histogram can separate a structured domain from a rearrangement of the same symbols. On a
corpus of period sixteen the histogram predicts one alignment in 65536 survives four anchors and one
in sixteen actually does, a factor of 4096. That factor is not a surprise and not a defect: reading
the corpus for coherence before the search predicts it in closed form as $2^{(k-1)H_2}$, from one
pass over the lags, at no cost.

What follows is the construction, the method as it is actually run, the ordering question that is
posited and unmeasured, and the vocabulary, including which words here belong to a field and which
this work minted.
