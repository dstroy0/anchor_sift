\chapter*{Abstract}

Measure the null under the same conditions as the effect.

Every claim withdrawn here, and two taken from the published literature, broke that one rule.
Five of them are the same error: the largest of $131{,}072$ dependency cells compared against a
threshold built for a single test. The maximum of $N$ standard normal draws sits near
$\sqrt{2 \ln N}$ whether or not anything is there, which for this matrix peaks at $4.85$ against a
Bonferroni threshold of $5.327$. Half a standard error separates the alarm from the null.
A fluctuation crosses it now and then and reads as a discovery.

Four anchors here are analytic. The round is a bijection exactly. All four
mixing functions have rank $32$ and kernel $0$ over $\mathrm{GF}(2)$. The narrowed rank deficit is
$\deg \gcd(p(x),\, x^w + 1)$, agreeing with elimination at eight widths. And a $\mathrm{GF}(2)$
linear variant is pinned at $0.5$ at every round by algebra, reading $0.50000000$ at rounds $8$,
$23$, $40$ and $64$. That control caught two errors it was not built for.

Reading every cell instead of the largest finds a pattern the maximum cannot see. Taking each cell
of the matrix to its class $(\text{input bit} - \text{output bit}) \bmod 32$ puts $4096$ cells
behind each number. With what every class has in common taken out, the classes that stand are $0$,
$6$, $11$, $25$ and $31$, and each is a named operation: the diagonal, the three rotation amounts of
$\Sigma_1$, and $-1 \bmod 32$ for the carry. The ranking is the same at every depth from round $6$
to round $19$. What decays at $1/32$ per round is the part every class has in common.

The null is the stronger half. Past round twenty-three the fold reads $2.09$, $2.07$ and $2.04$
against a null peak of $2.63$, at three depths, with roughly sixty-four times the sensitivity the
maximum had, finding nothing. The wall in the chaining state is the chain length plus two, derived
and never searched for, and integer addition is the sole source of algebraic degree while the two
nonlinear Boolean functions supply almost none of it.

Everything else here is a measurement, and every measurement states the sample size it was
made at.
