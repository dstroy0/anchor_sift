\chapter{The record of configurations}

This chapter states how each configuration of the arbitrary-precision arithmetic is recorded: what
is claimed of it, what would refute the claim, and what settles it. It also states the constraints
every entry is bounded by. The entries are measured and repeatable; they are not formal theory, and
several record the implementation as the thing measured.

\section{The schema}

Every entry carries five fields.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
field & what it holds \\
\midrule
Category & which class the entry belongs to \\
Hypothesis & one sentence, stated so it can be wrong \\
Configuration & every parameter needed to re-run it, none implied \\
Refutation & the result that would kill it, written before the verdict \\
Proof & what is run, what comes back, which way it settles \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Refutation is written before the proof.} A hypothesis with no refutation absorbs any result,
because nothing is excluded in advance. Only a claim stated sharply enough to be caught wrong can find
something.

\textbf{Configuration is complete or the entry is worthless.} A parameter left implied, the machine an
entry ran on above all, makes the entry unrepeatable and its result unattributable.

Identifiers are \texttt{E-\textit{category}-\textit{number}}, assigned once. A refuted entry keeps its
number and gains a verdict. Nothing is deleted, since a record of what fails prevents it being
retried.

\section{Standing constraints}

These bound every entry and are not re-argued in each.

\textbf{The search is precision bound, not time bound.} A relation whose coefficients run to \(D\)
digits cannot be seen below roughly \(D \cdot n\) digits of input, because the lattice cannot separate a
true relation from an accidental cancellation there. Depth is the only axis with no floor under it.

\textbf{A device multiply beats the host only above 1,024 limbs}, which is \(32{,}768\) bits. Below
that a device call is mostly transfer. Measured on one host and one device, and it moves with either.

\textbf{One region is closed.} Borwein, Galway and Borwein~\cite{src:Borwein} prove that \(\pi\) has no
Machin-type BBP arctangent formula in any base \(b \neq 2\) that is not a proper power. Searching there
returns nothing new, and it appears below only as a control.

\textbf{A candidate is not a discovery.} Surviving the screen shows only that a relation is not a
precision artifact. Most of the searched space is already mapped, and a surviving relation has still
to be checked against the compendium~\cite{src:Bailey-2023}.
