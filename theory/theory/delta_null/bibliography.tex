\AfterBibliographyPreamble{%
Every measurement in this paper builds on someone's published work, and this list
names them. A surname in a footnote is not a citation. Most sources here are held
in the registry \texttt{orior\_citations}, and for those the entry gives the file
and its SHA-256 hash. A reference points to the exact copy that was read. An
entry with no hash names the source by its publication details only.

A source is credited whether or not this paper agrees with it. Where this paper
reproduces a published result it says so, and where it fails to reproduce one it
says that too, under the same names. A failed reproduction depends on the original
as much as a successful one does.

\end{description}

against, and the hash answers that exactly. Two copies of the same paper are rarely
the same bytes.
