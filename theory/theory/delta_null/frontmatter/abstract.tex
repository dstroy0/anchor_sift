\bigskip

\noindent Measure how far an object sits from the most disordered arrangement of its own parts.

That is the entire method. Every discipline in this theory is that one sentence with a different answer to
what counts as a part: atoms in a cell, symbols in a corpus, bytes in a file, coordinates in a board
layout. The computational reference is built from the object's own parts, by itself. There is no prior
estimate, no training set, no model or neural net. The reference is the maximum entropy arrangement of
those parts, and the measure is the exact departure from it.

Two propositions bound what the search half can do. Any subset of a pattern's points is a necessary
condition for the pattern. A filter built from a subset never loses a true occurrence, whatever
picked the subset, whatever the alphabet is, and whether or not positions are ordered, so long as
each probe compares symbols, or ranks taken in one population with the pattern's. The converse fails for every proper subset. The exact compare is irreducible and every
arm has to verify what its anchors admitted. Soundness is free; the reading is not.

The search half is implemented in anchor\_sift at commit \texttt{1044ca6}, and Section 2.7 of the
first chapter states its guarantees with lines and conditions: exact counts for any legal probe set
on byte fields, and on rank fields of 256 classes or fewer under a transitive oracle; greedy
placement within \(1 - 1/e\) of the best on alignments rejected when every alignment is scored
(Nemhauser, Wolsey and Fisher, 1978); and a descent at most four levels deep, cut shorter by the
corpus, the candidates available or the count asked for.

This theory's proposed primitive instrumentation types are:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Identity.} The target set \(T\), measured against its own maximum information
    entropy. Unitless (Shannon, \emph{A Mathematical Theory of Communication}, Bell System
    Technical Journal 27, pp.~379--423 and 623--656, 1948; entropy at section 6, page 389, where
    \(H\) is a maximum at \(\log n\) with every \(p_i\) equal).
  \item \textbf{Null permutation identity}, \(\Delta_{\Sigma}\). The departure of \(T\) from the
    maximum entropy arrangement of its own parts. Its bit form reads the exact invariances of \(T\)
    that a drawn null does not reproduce.
\end{enumerate}

They are not interchangeable as evidence. The shift agreement detector recovers a period by how
often a shift agrees with itself, and it has external ground truth. The null permutation identity
takes a departure from the maximum entropy arrangement of the same multiset, and it has no positive
control from the outside.

The negative result is that collision entropy is invariant under permutation. An object and the
most disordered version of itself are the same cohesive coherent object and share the same
identity. No bound built from a histogram can separate a structured domain from a rearrangement of
the same symbols.

What follows is the most basic form of the algorithm. See the anchor\_sift engine workbook,
\texttt{theory/\allowbreak{}workbook/\allowbreak{}chapters/\allowbreak{}chapter\_\allowbreak{}anchor\_\allowbreak{}sift\_\allowbreak{}workbook.\allowbreak{}tex}
at commit \texttt{1044ca6}, and \texttt{docs/\allowbreak{}steering.md} in the same tree, for other
arrangements. We will work through the method as it runs, and talk through its
vocabulary, including new or nonstandard terms.
