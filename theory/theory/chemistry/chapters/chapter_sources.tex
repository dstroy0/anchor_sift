\chapter{Reference values and availability}

The specific magnitudes this research paper quotes are standard reference values: the covalent and
van der Waals radii, the Pauling electronegativities, and the bond lengths, including the backbone
values near $1.46$, $1.52$ and $1.33$ angstroms. They are held in crystallographic and gas-phase
spectroscopic tabulations and in the IUPAC recommendations. Each is quoted to the precision of
\emph{near} and is not cited to a specific table here; a value used in a measurement needs the table
it came from beside it.

The method, the protein and crystal representations and the screening are in the orior repository,
\texttt{https://github.com/dstroy0/orior}.
