\chapter{What a molecular reading would find}

\textbf{Nothing in this chapter is measured here.} It is the design chemistry adds to the method and
the predictions that design makes, stated before the reading exists. The method reads a protein and
a crystal but has no representation for a molecule itself, and no bond-length test oracle is
compiled.

\section{What a molecular reading needs}

Two parts are new and the rest is reuse, and one thing chemistry needs belongs to another subject and
is only consumed here. The element itself, its proton count \(Z\), its electron set, the Pauli
exclusion~\cite{src:Pauli-1925} behind the shell counts, and the periodic recurrence, is the atomic
structure, and it is stated once and shared by every subject that reads it. A second element table
under chemistry would be a second source of truth for a fact chemistry does not establish.

What chemistry adds is the bonding layer. A molecule needs a representation, atoms as points carrying
element vectors and bonds as the vectors between them, beside the protein and crystal
representations, since the three share what an atom is. The bond lengths need a test oracle holding
the tabulated lengths as facts, apart from the language family trees. Nothing else is added. The
octet runs on the screening's existing intersection, the disordered packing on the existing
shuffles, and the departure on the existing measures. If the reuse holds, no primitive learns that
chemistry exists.

\section{The predictions}

\textbf{First.} The octet as a necessary condition will refuse no real molecule and will prune
candidate arrangements, with the error one directional. A structure the octet admits may still be the
wrong isomer. A verification pass stays mandatory, and the count of survivors will move with the
composition exactly as the lattice cascade's does: a formula holding one element will over-admit
because every atom is compositionally alike, and a formula holding several will prune hard.

\textbf{Second.} A bond-length test oracle will be a positive control on par with the crystal cell
edge and stronger than any family tree, because valence fixes the value and a substance cannot argue
with it. A reading of molecular geometry checked against it will not be able to hide an error inside
a tolerance, for the same reason the backbone period can be called wrong the moment it leaves three.

\textbf{Third.} A crystal of a single element will return a large departure from the disordered
packing with nothing having authored it, placing chemistry beside the prime gaps and outside the band
every memoryless process occupies. A reading that took departure as evidence of production will be
wrong on every mineral, and this is the cleanest place to show that arrangement is not authorship.

\textbf{Fourth.} Reading a periodic property along \(Z\) will recover the shell structure only if the
partition it is read against comes from outside the sequence. The method reads a recurrence in two
composed steps, and both fix a single period. The period estimate scores a candidate period against
its own family of multiples, and a repeat at three is read as three and not as its harmonic six. The
periodic reference then builds the maximum-entropy background at that one period, binding each
position to the positions congruent to it modulo \(P\). A periodic property along \(Z\) is not one
period: the shell lengths are \(2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32\), a sequence that grows. No single \(P\) makes the
phase classes fall on the shell closures, and a reading restricted to one period reports a harmonic,
a single wrong period, or nothing.

A partition is stipulated, estimated by a sweep, or supervised from outside. It is not stipulated
here, since a judgment-picked boundary set is the bounding this research paper refuses. It cannot be
estimated, since a sweep for one period cannot recover a growing one, and that attempt is the trap.
It is supervised: the shell boundaries are ground truth the atomic structure carries, the same kind
of fact as the bond-length test oracle and reached from a second direction. The prediction is that
the real assignment departs far from its shuffle once the boundaries are supplied, and sits inside
the shuffle band to a single-period reading without them. On synthetic sequences, holding no element
data, a single-period reading departs on a constant recurrence and goes flat on a growing one, while
a reading handed the boundaries departs. This is the harmonic trap for one period, arriving again
where the recurrence grows.

\textbf{Fifth.} No reading here will need a bounded element set or a chosen cutoff radius, and any
that appears to will be measuring the bound. The bound will have to come off, and the reading will
improve when it does.
