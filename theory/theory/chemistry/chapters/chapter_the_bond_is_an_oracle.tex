\chapter{The bond length is an oracle}

\section{A fact, not a consensus}

The engine keeps its outside answers in one part, the oracle, and grades them. A family tree of
languages is a reconstruction argued from cognates. Agreement with it is agreement with a
scholarly consensus and where the two disagree nothing in the engine can say which is wrong. A
published crystal cell edge is the other kind: somebody measured it, refereed it and wrote it down,
and the instrument either recovers it or does not. The bond length is that second kind and more so.
Valence fixes it whatever anyone believes. A carbon-carbon single bond is near $1.54$ angstroms, a
double near $1.34$, a triple near $1.20$, and a substance does not get a vote.

This is why the oracle README already reserves chemistry a directory. Bond lengths get their own
place when they arrive, apart from the family trees, because a bond length is a fact and a family
tree is a consensus and the two must not share a namespace. The value the engine consults for a
protein already comes from this table: the backbone bonds are near $1.46$, $1.52$ and $1.33$
angstroms, nitrogen to alpha carbon, alpha carbon to carbon, and the peptide carbon to the next
nitrogen. None of those three was measured by the instrument that reads the backbone. They were the
answer it was checked against.

\section{Two domains already rest on it}

The engine has a reader for a protein and a reader for a crystal, and both stand on chemistry without
naming it. The protein backbone is a chain of bonds whose lengths cycle with a period of three,
because the three bond types repeat down the chain, and that period is fixed by chemistry before the
chain is measured. It is the first positive control in the whole work with an answer not produced by
the sample: a detector that returns anything other than three there is wrong, and no argument
afterward can save it. The three came from valence, and valence is chemistry.

The crystal is the same fact tiled. A cell edge is a repeat distance, and what sets the repeat is the
sizes of the atoms and the lengths of the bonds between them, packed into the smallest cell that
reproduces under translation. The instrument recovers $453$ of $453$ published edges inside one
voxel, and the thing it is recovering is a bond geometry stacked. So chemistry is not a third domain
beside the protein and the crystal. It is the layer under both, the building blocks and the one rule
that fix the lengths those two readers spend their time recovering.

\section{The fact is what makes the trap visible}

An oracle that fixes a period exactly is also what exposes a detector's own failure. A sequence of
period $P$ agrees with itself as well at $2P$ and $3P$. A detector that takes the tallest lag
reports a harmonic about as often as the period, and this has bitten three parts of the engine that
did not know about each other. It was caught on the protein backbone precisely because chemistry
said the answer was three: a reader returning six or nine could be called wrong on the spot. Without
the fixed answer the harmonic passes as a clean result, a confident number with a factor of two that
nothing flags.

That is the argument for chemistry as an oracle stated from the other side. Its value is not only
that it supplies an answer, but that the answer is exact and external. An instrument checked
against it cannot hide an error inside a tolerance. A bond length carries no spread that a detector's
mistake can shelter in. It is the hardest test in the tree because it is the one the sample can least
argue with.
