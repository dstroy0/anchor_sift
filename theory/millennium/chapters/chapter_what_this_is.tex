\chapter{What this is}

\textbf{Purpose:} State what this book records, what authorized it, and the boundary of what it
claims. \textbf{Scope:} \texttt{theory/\allowbreak{}millennium/\allowbreak{}}, and the corpus at
\texttt{Downloads/\allowbreak{}millenium/\allowbreak{}} as it stood on 2026-09-11.

\section{The book}

This is a reconnaissance record. It was written on 2026-09-11 in one session, after the corpus
below was assembled and a direct instruction was given to check one specific claim and write the
result down.

It holds four things. The corpus, with what each document actually is as against what its filename
says. The state of the Navier--Stokes problem, which changed three days before this book was
started. The reading of that change against the official problem statement and against the Lean
formalization published with it. And a recommendation, not this book's own, about which of the
remaining problems the project's apparatus could be pointed at.

It holds no attack on any problem. Nothing here was measured, nothing was run, and no line of
attack was begun. A book that opened with a method would be claiming that the method reaches
something, and no such claim is made or supported here.

\section{What is not claimed}

\textbf{No priority is claimed in this book and none is wanted.} That sentence is the same one
\texttt{theory/\allowbreak{}crystallography} states about its own construction, and it is repeated
here deliberately and for the same reason.

The reading of the literature is what is missing. For each of the five open problems in the corpus,
somebody has to work through the field behind it and write down what that field already says, and
that has not been done here. Until it is, nothing in these pages is a claim to a new method and
nothing in them is an attribution either.

There is an additional reason to say this plainly in this particular book. A priority dispute is
running in the field the Navier--Stokes chapter describes. That dispute was not investigated for
this book, no position on it is taken, and nothing in the chapter should be read as evidence for
either side of it. The chapter records what two documents say. It does not record who arrived
first, because that question was not examined.

\section{The rail this was written under}

Five rules were applied, and each is stated because it constrains what the later chapters are
allowed to say.

\textbf{Never a citation nobody has read.} A source is cited here only where it was opened. Where
a fact arrived by report rather than by reading, the chapter says so in the sentence that carries
the fact.

\textbf{A peer's reading is not a second witness.} Three sessions contributed to this book. Where
one of them supplied a fact, the fact was checked against the file or the document rather than
recorded on their word. Two such checks changed the answer and both are in the last chapter.

\textbf{Mark a gap in the sentence that states it.} Not in a footnote and not in an appendix. The
Navier--Stokes chapter carries a section listing what was not verified, and the individual
unverified points are also marked where they occur.

\textbf{A withdrawn claim stays on the page with what killed it.} The last chapter is that ledger.
Three claims were withdrawn in the course of writing this book, two of them this work's own.

\textbf{Draw the bar, never derive it.} A threshold reasoned out from a distribution is a threshold
whose forgotten variance sources all push one way. This rule arrives in this book as a correction
rather than as a precaution: the null offered for the recommended measurement was derived, its
author withdrew it, and the drawn replacement is what the toolkit chapter records.

\section{Whose decisions these were}

Scope was not this book's to set. Which problem, whether the effort exists, and whether anything
here was written at all were Douglas Quigg's decisions, and the instruction that produced this book
was his. The recommendation in the toolkit chapter is the project architect's, recorded as a
recommendation for him to rule on and not as a decision taken.

One structural decision was deliberately not taken here. The shared preamble declares theorem,
lemma, proposition and definition environments numbered within chapter, and the existing books do
not use them: they state propositions in prose and number them by hand. Adopting the declared
environments would reach every book through the shared preamble, so this book follows the existing
practice rather than changing a convention that is not its to change.
