\chapter{Particle physics: the engine on the atom and the Standard Model}

\textbf{This chapter is a case study, and the full treatment is a book of its own at
\texttt{theory/\allowbreak{}particle\_physics/}.} It carries the engine's method to a domain it was not
built for, atomic and particle physics, and the point is what the method kept the same: an object
written as points carrying values, read by equality and by census magnitude, in exact arithmetic,
against a background drawn from the data, and checked by an answer from outside. The runs are under
\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}particle\_physics/}.

\section{The atom as an accumulation}

An element is the electrons of its neutral atom, each an electron a point at an integer quantum state,
the spin doubled to keep it integer. Two electrons at one state is a Pauli violation, and
\texttt{exact.contested}, the same domain-blind primitive that finds two elements on one
crystallographic site, finds it. Over all 118 elements the exact reading places $Z$ electrons at $Z$
distinct states and none is contested, so exclusion holds by a count and not by an assertion.

The differentiating electron of an element carries a group signature, its subshell type and its
subshell's population with the principal number dropped, and that signature recurs down the table. The
recurrence is the periodic law, and it is measured against two backgrounds: a shuffle that keeps the
counts falls to the coincidence floor, and a filling without exclusion collapses to a ladder with no
structure at all. The shell closures fall out of the signatures at $Z$ equal to 2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86
and 118, the noble gases, and the gaps between them are the row lengths 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32, read with
no row told to the reader.

\section{The trajectory, exact}

The Bohr orbit of a one-electron ion is an exact rational: a radius $n^2/Z$ in Bohr radii and an energy
$-Z^2/n^2$ in Rydbergs, with the quantum number an exact integer and nothing rounded. Within a spectral
series the physical constant cancels, and a line ratio is a pure rational the model predicts with no
measurement in it, the Balmer ratios reading $20/27$, $125/189$ and $5/8$ against the first line.

\section{The particles}

The confirmed Standard Model particles, six quarks, six leptons and five force carriers, are written by
exact integer quantum numbers, charge in thirds and spin doubled, with no mass in the reading. Three
things emerge. The three generations recur with identical quantum numbers, one structure seen three
times as a periodic group was one signature seen down a column. Each generation's electric charge, with
every quark counted in its three colors, sums to exactly zero, the anomaly-free condition and the reason
an atom is neutral. And the free particles, the leptons and the bosons, carry a charge that divides by
three while the six quarks do not, the exact statement of why a quark is never seen alone.

\section{The oracle, and the discipline}

The ideal Madelung filling is a model, and it is held against the measured ground states from the NIST
Atomic Spectra Database. The oracle covers all 118 elements, 108 measured through hassium and 10
predicted above it and marked so, and reads off the 20 aufbau exceptions where a real atom fills against
the order, chromium and copper and the lanthanides and actinides among them, with most elements agreeing
as the positive control.

The whole subject steers by census magnitude and by equality, and never by a bound. The sift narrows the
candidate periods with the rarest anchor first, a lag is a difference between two agreeing places or it
is not, and no mass and no tolerance enters. A bound on a mass or a distance was the one decision the
domain refused, and refusing it is what kept every reading exact.
