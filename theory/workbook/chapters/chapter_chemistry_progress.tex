\chapter{Chemistry: progress and what is unmeasured}

Working notes for the chemistry subject, dated 2026-09-17. Kept here because a claim recorded beside
what it still lacks is the only check available on work with no reviewer, and because the subject is
mostly design and the design should be scrutinized before a reader is written to it.

\section*{The hypothesis}

Chemistry is the general question with the atom as the part: how far an arrangement sits from the most
disordered arrangement of its own atoms. The building block is the atom, a point carrying an element
vector; the rule that makes an arrangement non-random is valence; and the bond length valence fixes is
an oracle of the strongest kind, a fact and not a consensus. The protein backbone and the crystal
cell edge both rest on it. Chemistry is the layer under two existing subjects and not a third
beside them. The full statement is in \texttt{theory/chemistry}.

\section*{The boundary that was found before anything was committed}

The first thing measured was a collision, not a result. The particle-physics subject transcribes the
element ledger, \(Z\) and the electron set and the periodic recurrence, and chemistry consumes exactly
that. Two transcriptions of one ledger is two sources of truth for a fact neither chemistry nor a
protein established. The boundary is: the atomic structure is authored once in a shared
\texttt{representation/atom} home, and chemistry adds only the bonding layer, valence and bond order
and the bond-length oracle, on top of it. The engine holds any commit that would duplicate the
ledger. This was agreed before the element data was written, the outcome that boundary
existed to produce.

\section*{What runs today}

Four stages of the pipeline run on the primitives already in the tree, each graded by what it emitted
and each agreeing with its prediction.

\texttt{examples/chemistry/1\_represent/build\_molecules.py} builds seventeen molecules as atoms
carrying an element and bonds carrying an order, and octet-gates the catalog: all seventeen close, the
positive control on a hand-built table. It builds the connectivity and not the geometry, because a
bond's length is the oracle fact that is not entered yet. The reading it delivers is that a formula
does not fix a molecule: ethanol and dimethyl ether are both \texttt{C2H6O} and both close. The
formula is a label and not the structure.

\texttt{examples/chemistry/3\_reference/two\_nulls\_two\_questions.py} draws two nulls, each deleting a
different property. Permuting which element sits at which site deletes the element-to-site match, and
the octet departs from it on all five molecules read: the real molecule closes and most permutations
do not. A degree-preserving rewire deletes the connectivity while holding each atom's degree at its
valence, and the octet closes on every rewire on all five, including the self-bonded graphs that are
not molecules. So the octet carries which element sits where and carries nothing about which atoms are
joined.

\texttt{examples/chemistry/4\_measure/a\_histogram\_cannot\_see\_structure.py} reads collision entropy,
which is computed from the atom counts alone and so is permutation invariant. A molecule and every
rearrangement of its atoms carry the identical value to machine precision, worst departure
\(0.0\mathrm{e}{+}00\), and the two isomers read the same number. A histogram reads the formula and stops
there. Arrangement is left to the sift and the choice among isomers to a geometry the bond-length
oracle carries.

\texttt{examples/chemistry/5\_sift/valence\_is\_a\_necessary\_condition.py} reads the octet as the sift
proposition on chemistry: every atom of a real molecule closes. The octet refuses no molecule and
prunes the rest, one directional error. Over eight real molecules all eight close, each above a drawn
null band of 0.030 to 0.307, the null being the fraction of element-label permutations over the same
bond graph that still close across 400 shuffles. The two negative controls, carbon with five hydrogens
and bonded helium, are both refused. Two routes are read and shown to disagree: the handshake sum
holds on a mis-wired peroxide the per-atom octet refuses. Neither route is the other twice. The
sampled null rates track the arithmetic by hand: water near one permutation in three, methane near one
in five, ethyne near one in six.

\section*{What is unmeasured, and stated so}

The geometric molecule reader does not exist: the four stages above read connectivity, and no stage
places an atom in space. That reader belongs in \texttt{representation/structure} beside the protein
and crystal readers, imports the shared element ledger, and needs the bond-length oracle in
\texttt{oracle/chemistry}, which does not exist either. The shared ledger in
\texttt{representation/atom} does not exist yet. Stage one holds only chemistry's own valence layer
and transcribes no element identity. The periodic-property reading is a prediction with a known trap
in front of it: the period lengths are \(2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32\), not one number. A detector taking the
tallest lag will report a harmonic, and the shell boundaries have to be read as a partition instead.
The octet stages prove a necessary condition, not a sufficient one; which isomer a valid formula
becomes is left to a geometry measure and is not claimed here. Until the shared ledger, the
\texttt{representation/structure} reader and \texttt{oracle/chemistry} land, stage two, the geometric
part of stage four and stage six are design and are named as design.

\section*{A refutation kept}

The tempting strong claim, that a departure from the disordered background measures human production,
fails in chemistry as it failed on the prime gaps. A single-element metal packs into a lattice of
enormous order that no person arranged. So chemistry belongs with the primes, not with the prose:
arrangement is not authorship, and a reading that took the departure as evidence of design would be
wrong on every mineral in the ground. Recorded here so it is not rediscovered as a result later.
