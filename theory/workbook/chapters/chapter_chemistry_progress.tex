\chapter{Chemistry: progress and what is unmeasured}

Working notes for the chemistry subject, dated 2026-09-16. Kept here because a claim recorded beside
what it still lacks is the only check available on work with no reviewer, and because the subject is
mostly design and the design should be scrutinized before a reader is written to it.

\section*{The hypothesis}

Chemistry is the general question with the atom as the part: how far an arrangement sits from the most
disordered arrangement of its own atoms. The building block is the atom, a point carrying an element
vector; the rule that makes an arrangement non-random is valence; and the bond length valence fixes is
an oracle of the strongest kind, a fact rather than a consensus. The protein backbone and the crystal
cell edge both rest on it, so chemistry is the layer under two existing subjects and not a third
beside them. The full statement is in \texttt{theory/chemistry}.

\section*{The boundary that was found before anything was committed}

The first thing measured was a collision, not a result. The particle-physics subject transcribes the
element ledger, $Z$ and the electron set and the periodic recurrence, and chemistry consumes exactly
that. Two transcriptions of one ledger is two sources of truth for a fact neither chemistry nor a
protein established, so the boundary is: the atomic structure is authored once in a shared
\texttt{representation/atom} home, and chemistry adds only the bonding layer, valence and bond order
and the bond-length oracle, on top of it. The engine holds any commit that would duplicate the
ledger. This was agreed before the element data was written, which is the outcome that boundary
existed to produce.

\section*{What runs today}

One demonstration runs on the primitives already in the tree,
\texttt{examples/chemistry/5\_sift/valence\_is\_a\_necessary\_condition.py}. It reads the octet as the
sift proposition on chemistry: every atom of a real molecule closes, so the octet refuses no molecule
and prunes the rest, one directional error. Measured over eight real molecules, all eight close, and
each sits above a drawn null between 0.03 and 0.31, the null being the fraction of element-label
permutations over the same bond graph that still close. The two negative controls, carbon with five
hydrogens and bonded helium, are both refused. Two routes are read and shown to disagree: the
handshake sum holds on a mis-wired peroxide the per-atom octet refuses, so neither route is the other
twice. The null rates match the arithmetic by hand: water passes one permutation in three, methane
one in five, ethyne one in six.

\section*{What is unmeasured, and stated so}

Nothing in \texttt{theory/chemistry} beyond that one script has been measured. The molecule reader
does not exist, the bond-length oracle does not exist, and the periodic-property reading is a
prediction with a known trap in front of it: the period lengths are $2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32$, not one
number, so a detector taking the tallest lag will report a harmonic, and the shell boundaries have to
be read as a partition instead. The octet demonstration proves a necessary condition, not a
sufficient one; which isomer a valid formula becomes is left to a measure and is not claimed here.
Until the shared ledger and the two new engine directories land, the remaining pipeline stages are
design and are named as design.

\section*{A refutation kept}

The tempting strong claim, that a departure from the disordered background measures human production,
fails in chemistry as it failed on the prime gaps. A single-element metal packs into a lattice of
enormous order that no person arranged. So chemistry belongs with the primes, not with the prose:
arrangement is not authorship, and a reading that took the departure as evidence of design would be
wrong on every mineral in the ground. Recorded here so it is not rediscovered as a result later.
