\chapter*{Abstract}

This is the ledger. Every claim the work has made is here with the measurement that settled it,
filed by whether the claim held, and the refuted ones keep their entries.

No claim of novelty for the construction survives it. A necessary condition used to discard
candidates, with a mandatory verification pass and no missed occurrence, is the filtering paradigm
and it has a literature. The permutation null is Fisher's, from 1935. The maximum entropy reference
is Jaynes's, from 1957. Three fields have each held one of these for decades, and the cross-language
result was published by Montemurro and Zanette in 2011 over two hundred times the sample here,
fourteen years earlier. This work reached it independently and later, and found the precedent only
when it asked how such a thing could have gone unnoticed.

Three of the four regularities offered as evidence about human production were withdrawn after the
counterexample class was built. A memoryless process carrying a delimiter returns a Zipf slope of
$-0.978$ to $-1.332$ against a natural range of $-0.723$ to $-1.185$, a brevity correlation reaching
$-0.401$ where the strongest natural text here reaches $-0.364$, and a type count that overlaps at
three arms. Only dispersion against a permutation null survived, at $0.99$ to $1.01$ for every
memoryless arm against $0.48$ to $0.76$ for natural text.

The strong reading of that survivor is refuted too. The gaps between primes return $0.93$, outside
the band every memoryless arm occupies, and nothing authored the primes. The measure reads
arrangement, and human production was never intrinsic to it.

Several entries here exist because a number was quoted as a mean. The product rule error has a
median of $3.55$ on English prose and a maximum of $490{,}336$, five orders of magnitude apart, and
at twenty-five trials one needle contributed 96 percent of a reported mean of $19{,}684$. A protein
figure of $2.512$ was read as the fold and is not the fold. A resolution was read off an unshuffled
midpoint cut that landed on a paper boundary, and a resolution cannot rise as its sample grows.

What is worth the reading is the shape the corrections share. A claim that survived here survived a
null measured under its own conditions, and the ones that did not are on the page with what killed
them, because a document recording only what survived is not evidence.
