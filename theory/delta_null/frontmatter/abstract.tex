\bigskip

Measure how far an object sits from the most disordered arrangement of its own parts.

That is the method. Every discipline in this theory is that one sentence with a different answer to
what counts as a part: atoms in a cell, symbols in a corpus, bytes in a file, coordinates in a board
layout. The computational reference is built from the object's own parts, by itself. There is no prior
estimate, no training set, no model or neural net. This theory uses algebraic reasoning and
subtractive physics to leverage an information field's intrinsic processing power and knowledge
about what it carries to answer a question.

Two propositions bound what the search half can do. Any subset of a pattern's points is a necessary
condition for the pattern. A filter built from a subset never loses a true occurrence, whatever
picked the subset, whatever the alphabet is, and whether or not positions are ordered. The converse fails for every proper subset, so the exact compare is irreducible and every
arm has to verify what its anchors admitted. Soundness is free; the reading is not.

The two essential measurement types are from the object itself to its maximum information entropy
(Shannon, \emph{A Mathematical Theory of Communication}, Bell System Technical Journal 27,
pp.~379--423 and 623--656, 1948; entropy at section 6, page 389, where $H$ is a maximum at
$\log n$ with every $p_i$ equal), and the object under examination's null permutation identity.

They are not interchangeable as evidence. The shift agreement detector recovers a period by how
often a shift agrees with itself, and it has external ground truth. The permutation null measure
takes a departure from the maximum entropy arrangement of the same multiset, and it has no positive
control from outside.

The negative result is that collision entropy is invariant under permutation. An object and the
most disordered version of itself are the same cohesive coherent object and share the same
identity. No bound built from a histogram can separate a structured domain from a rearrangement of
the same symbols.

What follows is the most basic form of the algorithm. See

for other arrangements. We will work through the method as it runs, and talk through its
vocabulary, including new or nonstandard terms.
