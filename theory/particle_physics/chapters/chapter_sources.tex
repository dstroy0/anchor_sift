\chapter{Sources}

Each source is listed with what this book takes from it. The read sources are cited by path or by name.
The oracle's measured tables are now fetched and read; one database it will need for the absolute
spectral scale is named at the end and was not retrieved, so nothing is quoted from it.

\section*{The engine and the reading}

\textbf{The engine, six parts.} The construction this book reads with, represent, partition,
reference, measure, sift and oracle, and the rule that a symbol is reached through equality alone.
\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}python/\allowbreak{}README.md} and
\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}c/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}anchor\_sift.h}.

\textbf{Exact ingestion.} The primitive that keeps every digit and imposes no quantum from the reading
end, and \texttt{placed} and \texttt{contested}, the domain-blind readings the Pauli count is built
on. \texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}python/\allowbreak{}representation/\allowbreak{}exact.py}.

\textbf{The element ledger.} One transcribed table of atomic number, symbol and the Madelung filling
order, read by this book and by the chemistry subject alike.
\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}python/\allowbreak{}representation/\allowbreak{}atom/\allowbreak{}element.py}.

\textbf{The particle ledger.} The confirmed Standard Model particles as exact integer quantum numbers,
charge in thirds and spin doubled, with no mass.
\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}python/\allowbreak{}representation/\allowbreak{}particle/\allowbreak{}standard\_model.py}.

\textbf{The equality match rate.} The measure the recurrence is read with, and the coincidence floor a
shuffle reaches.
\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}python/\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}sequence\_match.py}.

\textbf{The runs.} Every figure in this book is produced by a script under
\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}particle\_physics/}: the atom as exact shells, the orbit as exact
rationals and the particle ledger in \texttt{1\_represent}, the quantum-number partition in
\texttt{2\_partition}, the two drawn nulls in \texttt{3\_reference}, the shell closures in
\texttt{4\_measure}, and the ideal against the measured ground states in \texttt{6\_oracle}. The
subject's \texttt{README.md} carries the figures beside the scripts that print them.

\section*{Reference data}

\textbf{Element symbols and atomic numbers.} The IUPAC table, canonical reference that names the
elements 1 to 118. It is standard and carries no single locator; the ledger transcribes it and states
so.

\textbf{The Madelung filling order and Hund's rule.} Standard atomic structure. The order fills 1s
through 7p and its capacities close on 118, which the ledger checks and does not assert.

\section*{The measured data}

\textbf{NIST Atomic Spectra Database.} The measured ground-state configurations, fetched by
\texttt{maint/\allowbreak{}data/\allowbreak{}fetch/\allowbreak{}fetch\_nist\_ground\_states.py} and read
by the oracle. It carries neutral atoms through element 108 and holds the aufbau exceptions the ideal
filling does not.

\textbf{The predicted superheavy configurations.} Elements 109 to 118 have no measured neutral atom, so
the oracle takes the calculated configurations, marked predicted and kept apart from the measured ones.
Source: Wikipedia, Electron configurations of the elements (data page), which compiles the relativistic
predictions past hassium.

\textbf{The Particle Data Group, at CERN.} The confirmed particle content, three generations and the
Higgs, and the measured masses, which the particle ledger records and this reading sets aside. Read
through the Standard Model overview that compiles it.

\section*{Named, not yet read}

\textbf{CODATA internationally recommended values.} The Rydberg constant, the Bohr radius and the
fine-structure constant, the measured hubs that set the absolute scale the exact rationals stand on.
The trajectory chapter's absolute comparison needs them and does not run, so they are named and not
quoted.
