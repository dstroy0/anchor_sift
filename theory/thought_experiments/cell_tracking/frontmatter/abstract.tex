\chapter*{Abstract}

Tracking cells through a developing embryo means detecting every nucleus in every frame of a
light-sheet recording and joining the detections into a lineage graph, with each division a fork.
This research paper proposes a tracker with no tuned parameter for the Biohub Cell Tracking During
Development task. It rests on three hypotheses, each stated with the measurement that would refute
it. First, a cell's low-degree spherical harmonic shape energies change less from frame to frame
than they differ between neighboring cells, and so carry identity. Second, a division or a death
shows as a departure in a record of change taken over the whole recording, which settles after a
division and not after a death. Third, an event displaces the cells around it with a far field that
points back to it, as a force dipole does in Stokes flow. The hypotheses supply costs to one
min-cost flow over the whole recording. None of them is tested here.
