\chapter{A constructed far end}

The measure used throughout this work places an object at one end and the most disordered
arrangement of its own parts at the other, and reads the distance between them. The far end is the
maximum entropy arrangement consistent with the object's counts, the reference that Jaynes made the
basis of statistical inference~\cite{src:Jaynes}. The reading is that distance and never a value on its
own. A dispersion of 0.28 says nothing without the reference it was taken against.

\section{A reference laid over a system}

The far end is constructed and then laid over the real system, and that overlay is an old working
method in mathematics. A square root of minus one, laid over algebra, gives alternating current and
quantum mechanics. Parallel lines that meet, laid over geometry, lead to the geometry of general
relativity. A quantity both zero and not zero gives calculus. A function zero everywhere whose
integral is one gives signal processing. Subtracting one infinity from another, which Feynman called
hocus-pocus~\cite{src:Feynman-1985}, gives quantum electrodynamics. Each met objection when it was
introduced, and each was kept for what the overlay predicted.

Every null in this work is that move. A permutation null is a corpus nobody wrote, laid over one
somebody did, and the difference is the reading. Scattering a protein's atoms through its own bounding
box builds a protein that cannot exist. The GF(2)-linear variant of SHA-256 is that function with the
part that makes it that function removed, and overlaying it gives an anchor that algebra pins at
\(0.50000000\)~\cite{src:Quigg-sha256}. A far end cannot be derived from the object, and it has to be
allowed instead of argued for.

\section{Building the thing expected to fail}

A null that has never fired is not a null. The memoryless arms are run on every sweep and return 0.99
to 1.01 every time. A flat corpus of one byte repeated is put through five instruments at once. A
generated ramp built with a width of 512 is measured and returns 511. The counterexample class that
took back three of the four regularities recorded in the workbook~\cite{src:Quigg-orior-workbook} was
built the same way. An instrument that cannot be made to say no is not reporting anything when it says
yes.

\section{Coherence to a reader is not coherence in the information}

What reads as sense to a person and what a structure permits come apart, and the workbook puts numbers
on the gap~\cite{src:Quigg-orior-workbook}. A perfect lattice carrying random values departs from
neither null, because a survivor needs a displacement and a value together and random values supply no
repeated pairs. A counter of period sixteen has a collision entropy of \(\log_2 16 = 4.0\) bits. The
quantity that governs cost is invariant under permutation and calls a perfectly regular corpus
memoryless, since it cannot see an arrangement at all. English prose measures 3.764 bits against a
memoryless control at 3.779. A memoryless process carrying a delimiter reaches a brevity correlation
of \(-0.401\), stronger than any natural text measured.

Each of those is incoherent to a reader and coherent as information, and intuition has no traction on
any of them. A possibility that reads as absurd is therefore allowed instead of dismissed, and allowed
so it can be pushed until it stops cohering. The place it stops is the finding. Nothing in this
research paper is believed on the strength of its plausibility, and nothing is dismissed for the lack
of it. The next chapter is the worked case.
