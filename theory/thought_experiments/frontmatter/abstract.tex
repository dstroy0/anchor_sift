\chapter*{Abstract}

These are the posits whose experiment cannot be built as written. They are kept apart from the
workbook for one reason: a conjecture about an unbounded carrier sitting beside a 582-row bench table
discredits both, and apart each reads as what it is.

This book runs the same construction as the rest of the work, on ideas instead of on corpora.

The measure puts an object at one end and the most disordered arrangement of its own parts at the
other, and reads the distance between them. Maximum entropy is the far end, and it is the stupidest
arrangement available: no order, no structure, nothing left but the counts. The reading is that
distance and never a value, which \texttt{src/\allowbreak{}engine/\allowbreak{}python/\allowbreak{}measure/\allowbreak{}dispersion.\allowbreak{}py} states plainly: a
dispersion of 0.28 says nothing on its own.

The far end is constructed and then laid over the real system, and that overlay is the oldest working
method in mathematics. A square root of minus one is logically absurd, laid over algebra, and gives
alternating current and quantum mechanics. Parallel lines that meet are absurd, laid over geometry,
and give general relativity. A quantity both zero and not zero gives calculus. A function zero
everywhere whose integral is one gives signal processing. Subtracting one infinity from another was
called hocus pocus by the man who did it and produced the most accurate prediction in physics.

None of those were believed when they were written down. Each was allowed, overlaid on a system
somebody already understood, and kept because of what the overlay predicted.

Every null in this work is that move. A null permutation is a corpus nobody wrote, laid over one
somebody did, and the difference is the reading. Scattering a protein's atoms through its own
bounding box builds a protein that cannot exist. The $\mathrm{GF}(2)$ linear variant in the SHA-256
book is that function with the part that makes it that function removed, and overlaying it gives an
anchor that algebra pins at $0.50000000$.

A posit here is not a detour from the method. It is the object the method needs, and it has to be
allowed instead of argued for, because a far end cannot be derived. Shuffling a corpus and positing
a demon are the same act.

A wild posit is written down here deliberately, and so is a control built to fail.

The construction gives intuition nothing to hold. The reference is built from the object's own parts,
so there is no prior to estimate, no training set, and no model of the domain to check a result
against. Nothing in that leaves a way to tell by thinking whether an answer is plausible. Two
practices follow, and both look wasteful from outside.

The first is to build the thing expected to fail. A null that has never fired is not a null. So the
memoryless arms are run on every sweep and return 0.99 to 1.01 every time, the flat corpus of one
byte repeated is put through five instruments at once, a generated ramp is measured and returns 511
where it was built with a width of 512, and the counterexample class that killed three of the four
regularities in the main book was built here. Nobody else found it. An instrument that
cannot be made to say no is not reporting anything when it says yes.

The second needs stating carefully, because it is the practice most easily mistaken for its opposite,
and because the reason for it was measured.

Our coherence is not information coherence. What reads as sense to a person and what a structure
actually permits come apart, and this work has put numbers on the gap more than once. A perfect
lattice carrying random values departs from neither null, because a survivor needs a displacement and
a value together and random values supply no repeated pairs. A counter of period sixteen returns a
collision entropy of 4.0, so the quantity governing cost calls a perfectly regular corpus memoryless;
it is permutation invariant and cannot see an arrangement at all. English prose measures 3.764 bits
against a memoryless control at 3.779. And a memoryless process carrying a delimiter reaches a
brevity correlation of $-0.401$, stronger than any natural text measured here.

Each of those is incoherent to a reader and coherent as information. Intuition has no traction on any
of them. A possibility sounding insane therefore has to be allowed instead of dismissed.

The gap gets proved again every time somebody here stops to check, and the checking is not the
expensive part. Declining to listen is. Every correction in the workbook cost less than the work that
went on top of the claim before it was caught, and the grid in the crystallography book stood for
weeks producing an error of 0.0124 angstroms and a bias nobody could account for, while the answer
was to stop imposing a voxel. The clock runs whether or not the result is being questioned.

Nothing in this book is believed. A possibility is allowed, which is a different act, and it is
allowed so it can be pushed until it stops cohering. The place it stops is the finding. The posit
is instrumentation aimed at its own boundary, and whether it was ever plausible does not enter.

Laplace's demon is the worked example. Nobody here holds that the demon exists. Allowing it and
pushing produced three claims: invariance under translation, permutation and change of coordinate
representation; that hitting the boundary once excludes a point permanently; and that the volume is
recoverable from its boundary. Two were already answered by machinery that existed before the idea
was written down. The third is where coherence ends, and it ends on the proposition that makes the
other two work: the converse fails for every proper subset, so the demon can refuse and cannot
predict.

That edge is worth more than the posit was. A filter that refutes cheaply and determines nothing is
the shape the whole construction has, and it is why soundness is free while completeness costs a
full compare.

Filed here does not mean untested. The delta null idea makes three claims and a bench already
answers two of them. Invariance under translation, permutation and change of coordinate
representation holds over 3,421 rows, spanning alphabets of 2 to 256 symbols, patterns of 1 to 32
points, dimensions 1 through 8, a rotated point set and a complex alphabet with irrational parts,
across 465,546 true occurrences with none refused. Hitting the boundary once permanently excludes the
point. That is the first proposition, and every arm in the kernel rests on it.

The third claim fails, and the idea was named for that one. Recovering the volume from its
boundary is the Laplace's demon half, and the converse of the first proposition fails for every
proper subset of the points. The exact compare is irreducible and every arm has to verify what its
anchors admitted. So the demon can refuse and cannot predict: an anchor set is a boundary, crossing
it is final, and nothing about crossing it says what was on the other side.

That is a real constraint. Nobody here reads it as a disappointment, and it is why soundness is
free while completeness costs a full compare.

What is not testable here is marked as such in the same sentence that states it. The holographic
claim in physics concerns a bound on the information a region can hold, nothing in this repository
measures that, and the resemblance is an analogy. The analogy earns its place only where it produced
the three claims above, two of which were checkable and one of which was already checked and refused.
