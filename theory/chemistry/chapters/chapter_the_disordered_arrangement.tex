\chapter{The disordered arrangement of the same atoms}

\section{The background is drawn, not assumed}

The question the engine asks is always how far an arrangement sits from the most disordered
arrangement of its own parts, and the most disordered arrangement is not a model to be posited. It is
drawn from the object by deleting one property. Take the atoms a molecule holds, keep their counts
exactly, and scatter their positions: the result is the least committal distribution consistent with
the composition, the constrained maximum of entropy, and it sits at a single point because entropy is
concave and a count constraint is linear. It carries no seed and no run-to-run variation, and a
substance with no arrangement, its atoms placed at random, returns a departure of nothing.

Chemistry makes this concrete in a way a text does not. The composition is the formula, the shuffle
is the same atoms at scrambled positions, and the departure is everything the bonds contribute over a
gas of the same elements. A tolerance is the wrong tool here and it is a tempting one, because a bond
either exists or it does not and a cutoff radius looks like the way to decide. The engine's record is
that every such bound has had to come back off. So the reading is not whether two atoms are within
some chosen distance. It is how far the observed set of distances sits from the set a random packing
of the same atoms would give, with nothing chosen.

\section{Order is not authorship}

There is a claim this construction had to give up once, and chemistry gives it up again from a new
direction. The claim was that a departure from the disordered background measured human production:
every corpus that departed had been made by a person. It fell when an unauthored sequence departed
anyway. The gaps between primes return a departure outside the band every memoryless process
occupies, and nobody wrote the primes.

A crystal is the same refutation in matter. A single-element metal packs into a lattice of enormous
order, its atoms on a repeating grid recoverable to one voxel, and no person arranged it. The order
is real, the departure is large, and the cause is physics minimizing an energy, not an author. So
chemistry belongs with the primes and not with the prose: it is a domain where the arrangement is far
from disordered and nothing produced it on purpose. A reading that took the departure as evidence of
design would be wrong here on every mineral in the ground. The departure measures arrangement, and
arrangement is not authorship, and the crystal is the cleanest place that difference can be shown.

\section{Which shuffle, and what it isolates}

The engine grades its shuffles, and the grades transfer. A full permutation of the atoms destroys
every arrangement at once and says only that arrangement is present, not at what scale. A shuffle
that keeps local neighborhoods and destroys long ones separates a bond, which is local, from a
lattice, which is not: the bond survives a long-range shuffle and the lattice does not, so a sweep of
the shuffle's reach locates where a structure lives. Scrambling within neighborhoods and keeping the
coarse layout does the reverse, and separates the packing of a phase from the identity of the atoms
packed.

None of these needs a distribution assumed for the disordered state, which is the whole point. A
model of a random packing could be wrong. The atoms of the actual substance, with their positions
deleted, cannot be, because it is the substance with one property removed rather than a guess about
what the substance is like. That is the sense in which no set here is bounded and no null here is
derived: the null is the data, shuffled, and the only quantity reported is the distance to it.
