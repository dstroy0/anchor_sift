# Mellesmoen_Kye_ICSNL61.oracle.tsv

Hand extraction of A Comparative Analysis of Stress in Northern and Southern Lushootseed.
Gloria Mellesmoen and Ted Kye, ICSNL 61.

Read off the paper by a person, not produced by any script. This file is the control: the reader
in corpus_script_extraction is checked against it, and where they disagree the reader is wrong
until someone reads the paper again and says otherwise.

Forms are written after two repairs, both of which the reader applies too.
The PDF puts a space after a stacked diacritic, so c̓ íx̌ id is the one word c̓íx̌id. A space after
ʷ is a real word boundary and is kept, which is what holds sčədadxʷ sʔuladxʷ apart.
The paper writes the ejective on ƛ and l two ways. č̓ƛ̓aʔ 'rock' is printed four times with
COMBINING COMMA ABOVE and once with COMBINING COMMA ABOVE RIGHT. One paper prints one word both
ways, and the second mark is normalized to the first.

where    the paper's own locator: a table, a numbered example, a section, a figure
dialect  northern, southern, both where the paper prints one form for the two, unstated where the
paper does not say, or the name of another language for a cited cognate
kind     citation    a word form the paper presents as attested
surface     a phonetic realization the paper prints in square brackets
transcription a line of connected speech from a recording
morphemes   the morpheme-by-morpheme line printed under an utterance
translation the English printed under an utterance, with the story and line it came from
winner      the tableau candidate the paper marks with ☞ and calls attested
candidate   a losing tableau candidate, a form the analysis rejects
underlying  an input form in slashes, an abstraction and not a word
impossible  a starred form the paper says the language cannot have
cluster     a consonant sequence cited on its own, not a word
segment     one sound cited on its own
notation    a template or a constraint name built out of the language's characters
mention     the name of a language inside an English compound
reference   a form sitting inside a bibliography entry
damage      a string the extraction produced that the paper does not contain
form     the form as printed, after the two repairs above
gloss    the paper's own English for it, empty where it gives none

Only citation, surface and utterance in a Lushootseed dialect are words of the language. A losing
candidate is a hypothesis the paper rejects, an underlying form is an abstraction, and a starred
form is a sequence the paper states the language does not have. None of those three is a word
anybody said, and all three would pass any test built on characters alone.
