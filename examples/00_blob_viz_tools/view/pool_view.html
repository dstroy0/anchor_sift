Concurrent miner processes on one card are time-sliced, and worse than time-sliced: two lose a third of the aggregate and it never recovers past one. So the pool is one device process, and parallelism belongs in streams inside it. Measured by src/import/hardware/device_scaling.sh, not assumed.
