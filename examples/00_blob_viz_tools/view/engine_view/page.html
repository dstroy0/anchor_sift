Prepared for the Kaggle Biohub — Cell Tracking During Development competition. A pure algebraic reasoning engine: branchless, memoryless, exact limb arithmetic. No neural net, no model, nothing learned and nothing held. It needs no internet connection and reads no dataset but the one handed to it. Any data prepared like the input set can be passed through by altering the .cfg — see README.md in the competition notebook. Non-clinical demonstration only. The engine renders this directly from what it sees. Move slide across the gradient between the sample and the engine's reasoning to verify the acceptance and rejection of that sample in real time, at any clock or perspective you need. That tunes the acceptance rate alongside the null permutation error curve written in the .tsv. The two terms are one integral, and it is the probability distribution for a given species and tissue composition. A fully working engine can be pointed at itself to floor the error within like tissue samples, which greatly improves candidate acceptance and rejection throughput.
