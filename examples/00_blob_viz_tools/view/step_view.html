How to read it. Bit 31 is at the left, bit 0 at the right, as the word is written. marks a differing bit. W is the schedule word this round consumes; S1 and S0 are the two big sigmas; T1 and T2 the temporaries. Only a and e are computed each round — the other six are copies shifted along, which is why a difference in b is just a's difference one round late.

Scrub from zero and three things happen in order: nothing differs at all while the flipped word is still outside the light cone; then a single orange bit appears in W and T1; then the sigmas turn that one bit into three at separated positions, the carry smears each of them upward, and within a few rounds half the bits are orange and stay there.
