Level readings are load-bearing. Roughly half of these came back flat, and they are published beside the rest on purpose: a flat reading from an instrument that detects signal elsewhere in the same run is evidence of absence, not absence of evidence. The nonce rows in particular are what rule out a shortcut — the distance landscape is flat at every lag, and hill-climbing on it loses to random search by 3.81 standard errors on an equal hash budget. The structure that is here sits in the version window and the timestamps, which is to say in the conventions and clocks of the people running the machines, not in the function they are running.
