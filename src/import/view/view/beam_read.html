Eyes on the State

Does a line integral through the SHA-256 compression state predict the final digest, where a region integral could not? 2048 nonces on one real header, read two ways at nine rounds, against a drawn null.

beam, 256 line integrals harmonic, 81 region integrals drawn null bar, 95th percentile of 200 shuffles

Why run this when the region read already said null

The earlier null was measured with six scalars, and five of them were power shares. A power share is invariant under rotation by construction, so that instrument could not see orientation at any strength. The state carries 256 numbers, one per bit. A six-dimensional rotation-averaged summary of a 256-dimensional object is not a test of the object.

A beam is a line integral, and it is not in the span of the region rows: stacked with the degree-8 harmonic map it takes the rank from 81 to 256, closing the whole kernel. It also reads passively, by occlusion, without perturbing what it reads.

Null at every live round before 64, on both instruments. The shaded band is rounds where the nonce has not entered the state: the nonce sits in schedule word 15 and round r consumes word r, so every state before round 16 is bit-identical across the sweep. Those zeros are construction, not evidence, and they are a control: variance there would be a wiring bug.

Round 64 is the positive control and it fires. The target is the leading zero count of state 64, so reading state 64 is reading the answer. A null there would have meant the instrument was blind and nothing else on this page could be trusted.

Where the eyes do win

On the one round that carries a signal, the two instruments do not read the same amount of it: the beam reaches 0.5361 and the harmonic 0.2570, a factor of 2.09 on the same state, the same target and the same null. That is the directional information a power share averages away, and it is a real property of the row type rather than a claim about it.

It does not help a miner. The early rounds have nothing for either instrument to find, and a better instrument pointed at an empty room still reads empty.

What the null is worth

The weakest limit across the live rounds is 0.100, a correlation below about that would not have been visible to this sample. The limit is measured by fading an injected correlation until it stops clearing the bar, not derived: three analytic floors in one session came out too low, and the rule since is to draw the null rather than compute it. The target takes 11 distinct values with a spread of 1.4575.
