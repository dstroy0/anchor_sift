\chapter{Shadows of the object}

\textbf{Purpose:} Look at the whole field instead of at a statistic of it, and let the picture
carry the argument that a table of numbers had already got wrong. \textbf{Scope:}
\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}bench/\allowbreak{}bench\_\allowbreak{}sac.\allowbreak{}cu} (the
\texttt{shadow} and \texttt{assay} arms), \texttt{tools/\allowbreak{}radar\_\allowbreak{}receive.py}.

\section{Everything here is a projection}

The object is three-dimensional (\(512\) input bits by \(256\) output bits by \(64\) rounds,
\(8.4\) million cells), and nobody looks at that directly. Every reading in this work is a
\emph{shadow}: the residue fold is the field collapsed along one axis, and the chi-square is the
same object flattened all the way to a single number. A shadow is cast from one angle, and until
these arms were built nothing here had looked from a second one.

That matters more than it sounds, because the projection-slice theorem says a shadow's transform is
a slice through the object's transform. Shadows are not impressions of the thing; they are the
thing, along a plane. Enough of them reconstruct it.

\textbf{Every row is the same row.} There is nothing further to it. The reading that produced the ridge
took the largest of these thirty-two and reported its amplitude in standard errors, without ever
subtracting what all thirty-two share, and what they share is nearly everything they have.

Two things put that offset into every class at once. The avalanche is incomplete at these depths.
And the message schedule imposes a causal boundary: round \(t\) consumes \(W_t\), so message word
\(w\) cannot affect the state before round \(w+1\), and until then flipping any of its bits changes
nothing at all. Neither depends on the output bit, so neither can concentrate in any residue.

\section{The waterfall}

The light cone is usually drawn as a staircase, and staircase is the wrong word. Nothing steps. Each
message word's influence \emph{falls}, from the dead plateau through a cascade of about seven
rounds, and each word begins its fall one round after the last. Sixteen overlapping waterfalls,
offset in time.

Aligned to its own wavefront every word falls the same way, to one part in \(10^{4}\) at the lip and

digit, being \(256(n-1)\) at \(n = 2^{18}\): not a large measurement but an exact structural
constant.

\subsection{What the plateau actually is}

It is tempting to read that constant as \emph{the input cannot reach the output yet}, and this work
did read it that way for a while. That is wrong, and the statistic says so if it is asked properly.

The projection sums \(z^{2}\) over the row, and \(z^{2}\) cannot tell a rate of zero from a rate of
one: both give \(z = \mp\sqrt{n}\) and \(z^{2} = n\). The sum is sign-blind. A bit that changes
nothing and a bit that flips its outputs \emph{every single time} land on the same plateau.

The evidence is a bit that has no business being there. Bit \(511\) (the most significant bit of
message word fifteen) still sits at the plateau at round seventeen, a round after word fifteen
has entered. It cannot be causally dead: flipping the top bit of an addend always flips the top bit
of the sum, because the carry out of bit thirty-one is discarded modulo \(2^{32}\). It flips bit
thirty-one of \(a\) and of \(e\) with probability one. Rate one, \(z = +\sqrt{n}\), and the same
\(z^{2}\) as rate zero.

\textbf{So the plateau is where the function is still perfectly predictable}, and being outside the
causal cone is only the special case where the prediction is \emph{no change}. The most significant
bits ride one round longer than the rest of their word for a reason that is arithmetic and not
causal: they never generate a carry, so nothing about them is yet data-dependent.

\textbf{The cliff is where determinism ends, not where causality begins.} Every number in this
chapter survives the correction (the constant, the waterfall, the shift invariance, the diagonal
at thirty-two bits per round), but the name was wrong, and a wrong name misleads further than a
wrong number does.

The register chains are the second waterfall and they are ducts, not mixers. \(b\), \(c\)
and \(d\) are copies of \(a\) delayed one, two and three rounds, with no mixing and no attenuation,
so information is carried forward intact. The duct length is therefore what sets where the wall
sits.

\section{Rocks in the river}

What the residue band shows is not the operations. It is their \emph{wake}.

The difference is what propagates; \(\Sigma_1\), the carry and the diagonal correspondence are
obstacles standing in that flow, and each disturbs it by being there. A residue class carries
amplitude because an operation upstream keeps moving bits by that distance, so the disturbance
piles up at that offset and stays there.

That reading is not a figure of speech, because the assay tests it directly and it is the strongest
result in this work. Take the rock out of the river and the wake goes: removing \(\Sigma_1\) drives
its classes from \(6.59\) to \(1.92\), and class six does not merely fade but flips sign, from
\(+6.83\) to \(-4.28\). Removing integer addition drives the carry class from \(+5.46\) to
\(-0.11\), because without addition there are no carries and nothing is transported at
\(-1 \bmod 32\) at all.

It also explains a result recorded elsewhere as a null. The Doppler scan found nothing moving
(drift within \(0.09\) of zero at all fifteen spatial frequencies), and that is not the absence of
structure. \textbf{A standing wave around a fixed obstacle does not drift.} The round function is
the same at every depth, and the only quantity that does vary with the round, the constant \(K_t\), was
measured twice by different means to contribute nothing to diffusion. The rocks do not move, so
their wake cannot. Zero Doppler was the mechanism reporting itself.

\section{What the picture cost, and what it bought}

The shadow arm was built to be looked at. It refuted the largest claim in this work, extended the
constant-decrement law two rounds earlier than it had been recorded, reproduced the ridge amplitude
from independent seeds to five significant figures, and, through a control built for an entirely
different purpose, explained the knee at round seventeen as the message schedule switching on.

None of that was what it was for. That is the pattern of this whole body: build the instrument,
then spend it on the truth.
